# LUMYD

**LUMYD (Let Us Mine Your Data)**

LUMYD is an AI-powered Business Intelligence platform designed to transform historical business datasets into intelligent insights, predictions, and recommendations.

---

## 🚀 Features (Planned)

- Dataset Upload
- Metadata Intelligence Engine
- Column Learning Engine
- Relationship Learning Engine
- Business Knowledge Graph
- Forecast Engine
- Recommendation Engine
- AI Business Assistant

---
